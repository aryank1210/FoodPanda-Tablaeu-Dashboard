# Foodpanda Customer Analysis Dashboard — Tableau

An interactive customer analytics dashboard built in Tableau Public to analyze customer behavior, spending patterns, and churn across a food delivery platform.

## 📊 Overview

This dashboard gives business stakeholders a consolidated view of customer engagement, restaurant preferences, and retention risk to support data-driven decisions around customer experience and marketing strategy.

## 🔑 Key Metrics & Insights

- **Customer Base:** 1,959 customers using digital wallets, with an average payment from wallet of 798
- **Average Order Frequency:** 25 orders per customer, with an average of 255 loyalty points per person
- **Gender / Restaurant Preference:** Breakdown of wallet usage across restaurants (Burger King, KFC, McDonald's, Pizza Hut, Subway) segmented by gender
- **City-wise Dish Pricing:** Heatmap comparing average prices of Burger, Fries, Pasta, Pizza, and Sandwich across Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore, Multan, and Peshawar
- **Age Group Spending by City:** Treemap showing average payment behavior across Teenager, Adult, and Senior segments by city
- **Churn Rate by City:** Identified Lahore (51.8%) and Multan (51.6%) as the highest-churn cities, notably higher than Islamabad, Karachi, and Peshawar (~47–49%)

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- **Tableau Public** — interactive dashboard design and data visualization
- Calculated fields for churn rate and average metrics
- Visuals used: bar charts, heatmaps, treemaps, and KPI summary cards
- Techniques: cross-tabulation by gender, city, and age group; retention/churn analysis
